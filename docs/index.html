Skill Sprout

Tools for growing readers. Reading fluency, running records, quick grading, and student reading history in one simple app.

Measure reading fluency

Open Fluency Timer, choose an optional student and passage, and enter the passage word count. Choose Start Reading, then Start when reading begins. Mark errors with Error and undo accidental taps with Undo. Stop Reading to review the values, calculate results, and save the attempt.

Manage students and passages

Add student profiles from Students. Save passage titles and word counts from More → Manage Passages. History shows saved reading attempts. Deleting a student also deletes that student’s saved attempts.

Get help

Report a problem or request help. Include your app version, device model, and steps to reproduce the problem. Do not include student names, reading records, or other private information in a public report.

Your data

Student profiles, passages, and reading results are stored on your device. There is no in-app account or cloud sync.
