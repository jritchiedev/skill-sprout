Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026. Applies to Skill Sprout 1.0.

Information stored on your device

Skill Sprout stores the student display names, passage titles, word counts, reading results, and assessment error timings you enter. These records are stored locally in the app’s database. You can use initials or nicknames for students.

No data collection by the developer

Skill Sprout does not transmit your student or assessment records to the developer. The app has no accounts, advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking. It does not record audio or request microphone access.

Device backups

Your operating system may include app data in device backups according to your device and account settings. These backups are managed by the operating system provider. Skill Sprout does not provide its own cloud sync or backup service.

Deleting records

You can delete saved attempts, students, and passages inside the app. Deleting a student deletes their saved reading attempts. Deleting a passage does not remove historical reading results. Removing the app removes its local database; copies in operating system backups may remain under your backup settings.

Use with children

The tools can be used by parents, teachers, and tutors to assess reading. The developer does not collect personal information about children through the app.

Support and policy questions

Contact support through the project’s issue tracker. Reports are public, so do not submit student records or private information.

Policy changes

This policy will be updated if app behavior changes. Optional future network features will be described before they are introduced.
